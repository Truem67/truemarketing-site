Privacy Policy

Johan van Eyssen, Founder & Owner, DoxSeal  ·  johan@doxseal.co.za
Governing law: Republic of South Africa

Effective Date: 14 May 2026

1. Introduction

DoxSeal is committed to protecting your personal information under POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013).

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

Name and email address for authentication only.

Usage Data

Number of seals and activity timestamps for service management.

Documents

DoxSeal does NOT collect, store or process document content. Only a SHA-256 hash is processed. Your document never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage your account;
  • Deliver the sealing service;
  • Send authentication codes;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or share personal information with any third party for marketing.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Resend — email delivery
  • Upstash — data storage
  • Render — server hosting
  • BSV Blockchain — hash storage only (no personal data)

5. Data Security

Account information is stored with industry-standard encryption and appropriate technical measures.

6. Blockchain Records

Only document hashes are recorded on the blockchain — no personal information. These records are permanent and public but cannot be reversed to reveal document content.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

  • Access your personal information;
  • Request correction;
  • Request deletion;
  • Object to processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

8. Information Regulator

Website: www.inforegulator.org.za
Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za

9. Data Retention

Data retained while account is active or as required by law. Request deletion by contacting us.

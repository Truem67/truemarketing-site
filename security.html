Security & Technical Overview

Everything an IT or compliance team needs to know before evaluating DoxSeal — what it does, what it stores, what it never stores, and how it's protected.

What DoxSeal is

DoxSeal creates a tamper-proof record of a document at a moment in time. It computes the document's SHA-256 fingerprint and anchors that fingerprint to a public ledger with a timestamp. From then on, anyone can prove in seconds that the document is unchanged since sealing — turning evidence disputes from days of email archaeology into a single verification.

Deployment — nothing to install

  • 100% browser-based (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) — no client software, plugins or agents.
  • No changes to your network, workstations or existing systems; no integration required to evaluate.
  • Sign in and seal — a first document can be sealed and verified within two minutes.

What is stored — and what is not

  • Your document never leaves your device — the fingerprint is computed in your browser; the file itself is not uploaded.
  • On the public ledger: only the SHA-256 fingerprint and timestamp — a fingerprint is one-way, document contents cannot be reconstructed from it.
  • Never on the public ledger: document contents, client names, policy numbers or any personal information.
  • In DoxSeal's secured database: seal records (fingerprint, timestamp, transaction reference) and user accounts, hosted on established managed cloud infrastructure.
  • Because no document contents or personal information are published, sealing is POPIA-friendly by design.

Security controls

Transport All traffic encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.
Authentication Two-factor authentication (one-time email code) on login; all authentication and authorisation enforced server-side.
Application hardening Strict HTTP security headers, locked-down CORS, rate limiting and input validation on all endpoints.
Administration Administrative functions protected by separate secret-key authentication, isolated from user accounts.
Availability Service health monitored 24/7 at five-minute intervals.

Verification is independent of DoxSeal

  • Seals anchor to a public ledger; proof does not depend on DoxSeal's servers, database or continued existence.
  • Any party — insurer, loss adjuster, court — can verify a sealed document free at doxseal.co.za/verify.html, no account required.
